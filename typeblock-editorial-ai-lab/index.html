LOADING — restoring the active local dataset before the layout starts.

Fudebam import reads only entries[]: content, createdAt, and captureOrigin. Records with hiddenAt or mergedIntoEntryID are excluded. Annotations, revisions, and AI review sessions remain sidecars rather than becoming Layout Entries. The newest 10 active Entries are selected, then placed oldest → newest.

The selected 10-entry snapshot and its Editorial Analysis are stored locally in this browser with IndexedDB. Reopening the same localhost page restores them without another API call when the Entry text and model are unchanged.

The layout groups consecutive Entries into 1–4 item Editorial Phrases. AI continuity and topic-shift signals are combined with a deterministic local text relation check; each phrase then selects a legal six-column composition while every Entry keeps its length-derived territory weight.
